    The journey’s questions, module by module

    M1What the journey asks of policy

    For each moment: who answers this question in our country today, and is it written down?

    • P0Which Onesan institution signs health documents on behalf of the health sector, and who in Taifafeki decides to accept that signature?
    • P1Is consent given once for the whole pilgrimage season, per facility, or per encounter — and can Kamal withdraw it while abroad?
    • P2What must the summary contain for a pilgrim — and who decides what is left out?
    • P3What should happen when a pilgrim arrives with no phone, a flat battery, or no QR code?
    • P4What protection should the passcode give — and is a passcode shared by every pilgrim of a country a passcode at all?
    • P5Does Onesa learn that Kamal's record was opened in Taifafeki, and by whom?
    • P6Who may open an unconscious pilgrim's record, on what legal basis, and how is it accounted for afterwards?
    • P7Should the host country send a summary of care back to the home country — and with whose consent?
