    Kamal Pilgrim · first Hajj · Malaysia

    Goal: Perform Hajj safely, and be treated properly if he is taken ill far from home.

    Needs
    • Health workers abroad who know about his insulin and his penicillin allergy
    • To decide who sees his summary, and for how long
    • Something that works from the one phone he carries
    Frustrations
    • Explaining his medicines in a language the clinic does not speak
    • Paper records that get lost or left behind

    How Kamal feels along the way

    A dashed line: not present in between · a point opens that moment

    Kamal’s touchpoints (7)

    1. P1 Three months before Hajj · A GP clinic in Kuala Terengganu

      The health check and the choice to share

      Has his health examination, listens to Dr Aisyah, and confirms on his wallet that Saudi health workers may see his summary during Hajj; sets how the link is protected.

      “So the doctors there will know about my insulin. Good.”

      Touchpoint: Pre-Hajj health examination; consent on the wallet
      Reassured Present
    2. P2 Before departure · Malaysia's HIE; Kamal's home

      His summary is assembled and signed

      Sees the QR code appear in his wallet as he packs.

      “Everything they need is in my phone.”

      Touchpoint: National health wallet
      Reassured Present
    3. P3 Second day in Mina · A Hajj health facility in Mina

      Taken ill in Mina

      Feels dizzy and weak in the heat; opens his wallet at registration and shows the QR code.

      “I feel faint. Show them the phone.”

      Touchpoint: Registration desk; wallet QR code
      Distressed Present
    4. P4 Second day in Mina · Registration, Hajj health facility, Mina

      Scanned and checked

      Sits and waits while the code is scanned.

      “I hope it works.”

      Touchpoint: Facility EMR with bar code scanner
      Worried Present
    5. P5 Second day in Mina · Treatment bay, Hajj health facility, Mina

      His record is read and he is treated

      Recovers on a drip.

      “I did not have to explain a thing.”

      Touchpoint: Facility EMR
      Reassured Present
    6. P6 The day of Arafat · A health tent at Arafat

      When he cannot show the code

      Collapses on the day of Arafat and is brought in unconscious.

      Touchpoint: Hajj registration number; emergency access
      Distressed Present
    7. P7 After the season · A GP clinic in Kuala Terengganu

      Back home

      Tells her, as best he remembers, what happened.

      “They gave me a drip… I don't remember what else.”

      Touchpoint: GP follow-up
      Neutral Present
