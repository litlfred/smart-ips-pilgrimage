    Onesa's health information exchange’s touchpoints (5)

    1. P0 Before the pilgrimage season · Lomaru and WHO GDHCN

      Keys and codes are shared

      Publishes the public keys it signs health documents with to GDHCN, and shares its national code systems with the WHO Terminology Service.

      Present
    2. P1 Three months before the pilgrimage · A GP clinic in Tanavu

      The health check and the choice to share

      Records the consent directive.

      Touchpoint: Pre-pilgrimage health examination; consent on the wallet
      Present
    3. P2 Before departure · Onesa's HIE; Kamal's home

      His summary is assembled and signed

      Assembles the IPS (conditions, medicines, allergy), issues a Verifiable Health Link to it, and signs it with a key registered with GDHCN.

      Touchpoint: National health wallet
      Present
    4. P5 Second day in Nalemi · Treatment bay, pilgrimage health facility, Nalemi

      His record is read and he is treated

      Checks the request against Kamal's consent and returns the folder and the IPS.

      Touchpoint: Facility EMR
      Present
    5. P6 The day of Toreva · A health tent at Toreva

      When he cannot show the code

      Releases the summary for emergency access, if Kamal opted in.

      Touchpoint: Pilgrimage registration number; emergency access
      Present
