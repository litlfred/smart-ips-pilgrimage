    Module M1 · The pilgrim's Hajj: the policy questions

    What the journey asks of policy

    For each moment: who answers this question in our country today, and is it written down?

    By the end, a participant can

    • Name, for each moment, who holds the answer today
    • Say which questions need the origin and host country to agree

    The questions, along the journey

    1. P0
      Keys and codes are shared

      Which Malaysian institution signs health documents on behalf of the health sector, and who in KSA decides to accept that signature?

    2. P1
      The health check and the choice to share

      Is consent given once for the whole Hajj season, per facility, or per encounter — and can Kamal withdraw it while abroad?

    3. P2
      His summary is assembled and signed

      What must the summary contain for a pilgrim — and who decides what is left out?

    4. P3
      Taken ill in Mina

      What should happen when a pilgrim arrives with no phone, a flat battery, or no QR code?

    5. P4
      Scanned and checked

      What protection should the passcode give — and is a passcode shared by every pilgrim of a country a passcode at all?

    6. P5
      His record is read and he is treated

      Does Malaysia learn that Kamal's record was opened in KSA, and by whom?

    7. P6
      When he cannot show the code

      Who may open an unconscious pilgrim's record, on what legal basis, and how is it accounted for afterwards?

    8. P7
      Back home

      Should the host country send a summary of care back to the home country — and with whose consent?

    Discussion

    1. M1 Trust P0 P4

      Who signs on behalf of the health sector, and who accepts a foreign signature?

    2. M1 Consent and access P1 P5 P6 P7

      What did the pilgrim agree to, and how is every access to the record accounted for, emergency access included?
