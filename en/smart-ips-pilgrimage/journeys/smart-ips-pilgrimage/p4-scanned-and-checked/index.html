    How they feel
    Pain point

    Without a signature check, the clinic cannot tell a genuine record from a forged or altered one.

    What makes it work

    The EMR verifies the signature against a key it got from the trust network, with no call to Onesa needed.

    Not yet specified

    • Not yet consistent: the concepts page says the default passcode is the pilgrim's birth date; the Malaysian implementation sets one standard passcode for all its pilgrims for the season.
    • The Clinical Encounter for Unplanned Care diagram draws no separate task for entering the passcode.

    Policy point. What protection should the passcode give — and is a passcode shared by every pilgrim of a country a passcode at all?
